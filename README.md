# Discover Syria · اكتشف سوريا

**Explore the history, culture, flavors, and hidden gems of Syria.**

A bilingual (English and Arabic) travel guide that a visitor can open from the moment they decide to visit Syria until they are back home.

**Live site:** https://zain1269.github.io/discover-syria/

## Status

This is an **MVP** and an **independent project**. It is not an official tourism website and is not connected to any government body.

- Many places are not included yet.
- Restaurant entries are sample data and are labelled as such on the site.
- Visa rules, prices and opening hours are left out on purpose because they change often. Check official sources before traveling.

## What is inside

| Section | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Destinations | 9 cities and regions: Damascus, Aleppo, Homs, Hama, Latakia, Tartus, Palmyra, Bosra, Maaloula |
| History and heritage | 11 sites, filterable by historical period and location, with notes on damage where it applies |
| Taste Syria | A guide to 12 Syrian dishes, plus sample restaurant listings |
| Experiences | 12 ways to spend a day, from old souqs and craft workshops to monasteries and the coast |
| Itineraries | Suggested routes for 1, 3, 5, 7 and 10+ days |
| Map | Every place on one interactive map |
| Travel guide and safety | Practical planning notes, useful Arabic phrases, and links to official travel advisories |
| My Syria trip | Save favorite places in your browser and come back to them later |

Also included:

- Search in English and Arabic
- Full Arabic version with right-to-left layout
- Works on phones, tablets and computers

## Built with

React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind CSS.

This repository hosts the built site as a single `index.html` file with the photos embedded, served by GitHub Pages.
